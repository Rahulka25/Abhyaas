About us

Discover the story, mission, and people behind Abhyaas Pre-University College.

Our story

At Abhyaas PU College, we believe every student carries a spark of potential waiting to be discovered. We go beyond textbooks and exam scores to nurture confident, curious, and capable young individuals ready to take on the world.

Located in the serene surroundings of Ujire, Dakshina Kannada, our campus provides the perfect environment for focused learning and holistic growth.

Our approach

Experienced faculty don't just teach — they mentor and guide, taking the time to understand each student's learning style. Paired with modern infrastructure and innovative teaching methods, we create an environment where students don't just memorize concepts, but truly understand and apply them.

Integrated competitive exam coaching

We offer integrated coaching for top competitive entrance exams — KCET, NEET, JEE, NDA, and many more — alongside regular board exam preparation. This means students prepare for both simultaneously, under one roof, with specialized faculty, regular mock tests, and personalized doubt-clearing sessions.

"At Abhyaas PU College, we prepare students not just for exams, but for life."

Join us, and let's build your future together.

Required documents

  • SSLC / 10th Marks Card (original + 2 copies)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school
  • Birth Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card copy
  • Passport size photographs (4 copies)
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (for scholarship)
