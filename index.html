About us

Discover the story, mission, and people behind Abhyaas Pre-University College.

📖

Our story

Abhyaas Academy was started at Belthangady in the year 2021 with a foreseeing vision by the Late Shri M Sadashiva Baipadithaya, along with his wife Smt K Meenakshi. Abhyaas emerged at the whims and fancies of such extraordinary visionaries, and their vision was fulfilled by their son, Mr Karthikeya. It ought to be recollected that the Late Shri Sadashiva was a teacher himself, and so was his wife.

Thus, Abhyaas Academy in Belthangady came into existence, where the main concentration was given to NEET, JEE and CET Long-Term coaching. Their son has brought the vision of his parents to the forefront by establishing Abhyaas Academy over a period of time, and has now ventured on a new journey by establishing Abhyaas PU College in Ujire.

Located in the serene surroundings of Ujire, Dakshina Kannada, our campus provides the perfect environment for focused learning and holistic growth.

🔭

Vision

Abhyaas College aims at imparting qualitative education, set in a tranquil environment which seeks to function through mutual love and utmost dedication. We cater and uphold the values of teaching ethics all through.

🎯

Mission

Our mission is to provide an exceptional learning environment where our students can enhance and hone their skills for a better and brighter future. We build leadership skills, creative and intellectual powers, and a lifelong passion for learning.

🌟

Our approach

Experienced faculty don't just teach — they mentor and guide, taking the time to understand each student's learning style. Paired with modern infrastructure and innovative teaching methods, we create an environment where students don't just memorize concepts, but truly understand and apply them.

Integrated competitive exam coaching

We offer integrated coaching for top competitive entrance exams alongside regular PUC studies — students prepare for both simultaneously, under one roof, with specialized faculty, regular mock tests, and personalized doubt-clearing sessions.

NEET JEE Mains & Advanced CET NDA NATA IMUCET UCEED NUCAT CA Foundation CSEET CLAT IBPS

In addition, we help our students prepare for Civil Service Exams:

IAS IPS KAS SSA FDA SDA

We also run Soft Skills Programmes and a Digitalized Language Club for effective communication, along with IELTS/OET classes for students planning to study abroad.
